About the Campaign

Confronting corporate accountability and state fund negligence in South Africa. An open-source archive documenting systemic delays across the RAF, COIDA, RMA, and private insurance.

Transforming Silent Administrative Abuse into Public Accountability

This platform was not built merely to chronicle a personal grievance. It exists as an independent, transparent, and unassailable digital repository dedicated to exposing the structural mechanics of institutional bad faith within the South African social security and corporate frameworks.

For over a decade, the individual behind this campaign—Elias—has navigated a complex, multi-front legal battle against the Employer (Valterra), the Compensation Fund (COIDA / Rand Mutual Assurance), and a concurrent Private Insurance Entity following a severe, life-altering occupational injury.

Instead of receiving lawful adjudication and statutory relief, Elias was met with calculated stall tactics, administrative file-swapping, and a coordinated strategy designed to exhaust a sick worker's resources until they abandon their constitutional rights. This website removes the shield of institutional anonymity. By publishing verified, physical facts, this campaign establishes a permanent archive of public accountability.

The Core Objectives

Our operational mandate is governed by three strict pillars:

1. Enforce Statutory Adjudication

Forcing the Compensation Commissioner, the Public Protector, and licensed insurers (RMA) to execute their lawful duties without manufacturing undue delays or stalling critical processes like the Section 91 Objection (Form WG.29).

2. Protect and Preserve Evidence

Utilizing secure, public infrastructure to lock down medical diagnoses, clinical imaging, and official records so they can never be quietly deleted, altered, or manipulated behind closed doors by corporate-contracted doctors.

3. Educate Vulnerable Workers

Providing an open-source litigation blueprint for thousands of South African miners, construction workers, and citizens who find themselves discarded by massive corporations the moment they suffer a workplace injury or contract an occupational disease.

The Reality of the "Institutional Army"

In South Africa, the state and corporate systems often rely on a "war of attrition." They know that an injured individual surviving on a minimal pension fund cannot afford decades of high-level litigation. They expect you to go away quietly.

When you challenge a multi-billion Rand mining corporation or a state-backed fund, you are not fighting a single office; you are fighting a coordinated "army" of legal counsels, corporate medical panels, and compromised administrative pipelines. They exploit loopholes, ignore their own 14-day tracking guidelines, misdirect claimants with incorrect forms, and weaponize time to beat you before you ever step foot into a tribunal or a High Court.

This platform changes the battlefield. By routing every interaction, every failure, and every piece of medical proof into the open light, we level the playing field. Money and corporate lobbying cannot bribe an unassailable digital archive.

Our Stance on Defamation and Legal Protection

Every document hosted within our Evidence Vault and mapped across our Priority Stakeholder Matrix consists strictly of objective, physical data. This includes stamped public protector files, signed specialist reports, registered SAPS case numbers, and official corporate emails.

We do not deal in speculation or emotional allegations. We deal in undisputed, empirical facts. This platform operates in absolute compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and utilizes the transparent spirit of the Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA) as a shield for public interest advocacy.

📄 Sample Template Structure: Form WG.29 Section 91 Objection (Excerpt)

FORM WG.29: OBJECTION IN TERMS OF SECTION 91 OF THE COMPENSATION FOR
OCCUPATIONAL INJURIES AND DISEASES ACT 130 OF 1993

TO: The Compensation Commissioner / Licensed Insurer (RMA)
FROM: [Claimant Name / Legal Representative]
DATE: [DD/MM/YYYY]
CLAIM REFERENCE NUMBER: [Insert Claim Number]

SUBJECT: FORMAL OBJECTION TO DECISION / ADMINISTRATIVE NON-PERFORMANCE

1. GROUNDS FOR OBJECTION:
   1.1. The Compensation Fund / Licensed Insurer has failed to recognize
        documented psychiatric trauma (PTSD) as part of the primary
        occupational injury profile, despite comprehensive medical evidence
        submitted via Form W.Cl.303.
   1.2. The statutory 14-day response window for acknowledging the
        Section 91 Objection has lapsed without formal communication.
   1.3. Administrative file-swapping (Claim ending in -57 crossed with
        Claim ending in -62) has been documented, constituting maladministration.

2. LEGAL CITATIONS:
   - Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act 130 of 1993, Section 91
   - Promotion of Administrative Justice Act 3 of 2000 (PAJA)
   - Constitution of the Republic of South Africa, Section 33 (Just Administrative Action)

3. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE ATTACHED:
   - Form W.Cl.303: First Medical Report in Respect of PTSD (Dr. Kewana)
   - Psychiatric Assessment Report (dated [DD/MM/YYYY])
   - Correspondence log showing 45 days of non-responsiveness
   - Screenshots of electronic claim registry showing file-swapping

4. DEMAND:
   The claimant hereby demands that the Compensation Commissioner / Licensed Insurer:
   4.1. Immediately recognize the psychiatric trauma component of the claim.
   4.2. Provide a formal written response within 14 days of receipt.
   4.3. Cease administrative file-swapping and consolidate all medical evidence
        under a single claim reference.

FAILURE TO COMPLY:
Should the Compensation Commissioner / Licensed Insurer fail to respond within
the prescribed statutory window, the claimant will escalate this matter to the
Office of the Public Protector and pursue High Court review under PAJA.

SIGNED: ___________________________
DATE: _____________________________
